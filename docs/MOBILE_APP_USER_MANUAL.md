# SPECTRON AgriAssist Mobile User Manual

## Sign in

1. Open **SPECTRON AgriAssist**.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Tap **Sign In**.

Tap the profile picture at the top of the app to update your profile or change your password.

## Main navigation

Use the bottom menu to move between:

- **Farms** — your farms and fields.
- **Live** — current sensor readings and charts.
- **Hardware** — controllers, Sensor Bases, and sensors.
- **Alerts** — items that need attention.
- **Access** — invite viewers or remove access (farm owners only).

## Create a farm

1. Open **Farms**.
2. Tap **Add Farm**.
3. Enter the farm name and confirm its location.
4. Tap the save button.

Open a farm to view its fields, crop information, weather, and alerts.

## Set up hardware

1. Open **Hardware**.
2. Tap **Set up hardware**.
3. Follow the steps to add a controller, connect Sensor Bases to fields, and check readings.

The app shows simple status labels:

- **Good** — working normally.
- **Needs attention** — action may be needed.
- **Offline** — the device is not communicating.
- **Waiting setup** — setup is not complete.

## Check crop conditions

Open **Live** to select a farm and view recent measurements. Open a farm or field to see crop stage, weather, and recommendations.

## Respond to alerts

1. Open **Alerts**.
2. Read the alert and recommended action.
3. Tap the available action to acknowledge it when it has been handled.

## Share farm access (owners)

1. Tap **Access** in the bottom menu.
2. Invite a viewer using their email address.
3. Remove a viewer when they no longer need access.

Viewers can see farm information but cannot change farms, hardware, crops, or access.

## Getting help

Use the small `i` buttons in the app for short explanations. If a device remains Offline, first check its power and network signal, then contact the SPECTRON support team.
